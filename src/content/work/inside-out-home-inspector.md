### What it was

Inside Out Home Inspector, a small family-owned business in central Maryland providing residential home inspection and radon testing services.

### My role

For seven years I ran the whole marketing function: brand, social media, and outreach.

### The outcome

I built a recognizable local brand backed by consistent five-star reviews, and a reputation that helped homeowners buy and sell with confidence. The business has since closed.
