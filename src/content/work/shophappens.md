## Overview

ShopHappens is an affiliate content site I've built and run since 2007. It helps people make sense of online shopping and the digital marketing tactics that stack up against them.

## Challenge

Online shopping gets more complicated every year, and most of that complexity is designed to work against the shopper. The site exists to make it legible.

## My role

Everything: the build, the content, the affiliate relationships, and the decisions about what the site should be as the landscape changed.
