### What it was

TD Ameritrade's MarTech Enablement and Data Architecture Lab, the team behind the marketing technology stack for a major brokerage: tag management, analytics, testing, and personalization tools including Tealium, ObservePoint, and the Adobe marketing suite.

### My role

I coordinated two delivery teams of MarTech engineers across a major brokerage's digital properties, through the TD Ameritrade and Charles Schwab merger.

### The outcome

We rolled out tag and consent management across more than 100 sites in the ecosystem and set up data layer governance, which gave the marketing teams analytics they could trust. I also found and closed privacy gaps before they could cost the business anything.
