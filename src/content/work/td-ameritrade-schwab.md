### What it was

TD Ameritrade's MarTech Enablement and Data Architecture Lab, the team behind the marketing technology stack for a major brokerage: tag management, analytics, testing, and personalization tools including Tealium, ObservePoint, and the Adobe marketing suite.

### My role

Scrum Master and project manager for the team from 2018 to 2021, coordinating Kanban-based work across two delivery teams and keeping communication moving across the enterprise. After the acquisition, I continued at Charles Schwab as a senior IT program manager focused on tag management and data architecture across legacy TD Ameritrade and Schwab digital properties.
