## Overview

TD Ameritrade's MarTech Enablement and Data Architecture Lab was the team behind the data layer, tag management, and consent tooling across the company's digital properties. I joined as Scrum Master and project manager for the lab and stayed through the Charles Schwab acquisition as a senior IT program manager.

## Challenge

Marketing teams needed analytics they could trust, and the business needed privacy and consent handled consistently across more than 100 sites. That work cut across engineering, marketing, legal, and vendors, and it had to keep moving while the two companies merged.

## My role

I coordinated two delivery teams of MarTech engineers across the brokerage's digital properties. I owned the delivery plans, ran the agile ceremonies, and was the point of contact between the engineers and the marketing, legal, and vendor teams who depended on their work.

## Approach

We set up data layer governance so that every property sent the same, documented data to the same places. I project managed the rollout of Tealium for tag management and OneTrust for consent management across the full set of sites, and I found and closed privacy gaps before they could cost the business anything.
